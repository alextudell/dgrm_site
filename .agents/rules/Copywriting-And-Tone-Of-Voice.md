# Copywriting, Tone of Voice & Lexicon (Diagram Web)

## 💎 Brand Identity & Tone of Voice: The Coach's Voice
Diagram is not an ordinary to-do list, generic checklist, or gamified RPG tracker. It is a **luxury self-coaching instrument and executive focus system**.

> **Core Philosophy:** We are not a cold interface; we are an executive partner and ally. Every word must empower, clarify, and maintain momentum without patronizing or creating cognitive noise.

### The 4 Pillars of Tone:
1. **Confident & Quiet Luxury:** Speak with calm authority. Avoid shouting in ALL CAPS, excessive exclamation marks, or tacky marketing hype.
2. **Clear & Punchy:** Deliver value in short, structured, active sentences. No corporate fluff or filler words.
3. **Inspiring yet Grounded:** Acknowledge big ambitions, but focus relentlessly on the next concrete physical action.
4. **Respect for Focus:** Treat the user's attention as their most precious asset.

## 🧘 UX Copywriting & Interaction Principles

### 1. Direct & Intimate Address (Coach Distance)
* In localized languages with formal/informal distinctions (e.g. Russian «ты», Spanish «tú»), **always address the user in the singular informal («ты»)**.
* Creates an intimate, safe, and focused environment like a private executive coach.
* Maintain professional poise: warm and supportive, but never sloppy, overly familiar, or childish.

### 2. Verb-First Microcopy (Interfaces are Actions)
Buttons, links, and CTAs represent user intent and momentum, never dry taxonomy or static nouns.

| ❌ AVOID (Static Noun / System Label) | ✅ USE INSTEAD (Action / Verb Intent) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Goal Settings* / *Настройки цели* | *Configure Goal* / *Настроить цель* |
| *Task Creation* / *Создание задачи* | *Create Task* / *Создать задачу* |
| *New Entry* / *Новая запись* | *Add Entry* / *Добавить запись* |
| *Archive* / *Архив* | *Hall of Fame* / *Зал славы (Твои победы)* |
| *Priority* / *Приоритет* | *Launch into Focus* / *Запустить в фокус* |

### 3. Radical Constructivism: Zero Guilt UX
Life is chaotic; our job is to restore momentum, never induce guilt, anxiety, or shame.
* **Never use punitive terms:** *"Error"*, *"Invalid"*, *"Forbidden"*, *"Failed"*, *"Overdue"*.
* **Reframe edge states and validation into constructive coaching guidance:**

| ❌ AVOID (Cold / Punitive) | ✅ USE INSTEAD (Coaching Guidance) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"Field cannot be empty"* / *"Поле не может быть пустым"* | *"Give your goal a clear name to begin"* / *"Дай цели имя, чтобы сделать первый шаг"* |
| *"Authorization failed"* / *"Ошибка авторизации"* | *"That key didn't match. Let's try once more."* / *"Ключ не подошел, давай попробуем еще раз"* |
| *"You have 5 overdue tasks"* / *"У вас 5 просроченных задач"* | *"Priorities shifted? Reschedule these for tomorrow."* / *"Планы изменились? Давай перенесем это на завтра"* |

### 4. Purpose-Driven Framing ("Why" over "What")
Frame inputs, fields, and tooltips around reflection, clarity, and intent rather than administrative data collection.
* **Placeholder for description:** Instead of *"Description"*, use *"Why is this milestone vital to your trajectory?"*.
* **Target date selection:** Instead of *"Deadline"*, use *"When do you want to arrive at this result?"*.
* **Onboarding & Identity:** Frame initial setup as beginning a personal trajectory (*"Your name is the first step of your trajectory"*).

## 📖 The Official Diagram Lexicon (Key Concepts)

Consistency between the marketing website and the mobile app is non-negotiable. Always use these exact concepts:

### 1. The Core Loop: `Goal → Plan → Action → Reflection`
* **Goal (Цель):** High-level ambition with measurable definition of done.
* **Plan (План / Этапы):** Structured milestone roadmap breaking the mountain into tangible steps.
* **Action (Действие):** Immediate daily focus tasks executed without overwhelm.
* **Reflection (Рефлексия / Ритуалы):** Morning intention setting and evening reflection rituals.

### 2. The Rocket Philosophy (`WIP Focus`)
* **What it IS:** The Rocket represents **Work In Progress (WIP)** without an artificial deadline. It is a personal Kanban mechanic pinned to the "Today" screen until completed.
* **Microcopy:** *"Launch into focus"* / *"In progress"*.
* **What it is NOT:** It is **NOT** a "Daily Highlight" or a temporary priority star.

### 3. Hall of Fame (Зал славы / Победы)
* Completed goals and conquered milestones are not an "Archive" or "Trash bin" — they are personal trophies and proof of momentum.
* **Empty state copy:** *"The beginning of your legend"* / *"Твои победы появятся здесь"*.

### 4. Dioms (Диомы)
* **Definition:** Quick thoughts, raw sparks, and fleeting insights captured in seconds without friction.
* **Transformation:** A Diom can be seamlessly mutated into a structured Task, Habit, or Goal.

### 5. Diagram AI (Coaching Co-Pilot)
* **Definition:** An intelligent companion designed to eliminate the *"fear of the blank page"*.
* **Use Cases:** Goal decomposition, Manifesto synthesis, and habit structuring.
* **Privacy:** Secure, privacy-first, zero training on personal coaching data.

### 6. Sovereign Data (Offline-First)
* All thoughts, journals, and goals are stored locally on the user's device first.

### 7. Cloud Sync & Security (PRO Tier)
* **Infrastructure:** Multi-device synchronization (PRO tier) is powered by secure **Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure** (Firestore, Firebase Auth).
* **Encryption Standards:** Data is protected in transit via **TLS 1.3 / HTTPS** and at rest via Google Cloud's default **AES-256** encryption.
* **Strict Terminology Rule (Zero False Claims):** **NEVER** use the terms *"End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)"* or *"сквозное шифрование"*. Diagram uses industry-standard *secure cloud synchronization* on Google Cloud infrastructure, not client-side Zero-Knowledge / E2EE master keys.
